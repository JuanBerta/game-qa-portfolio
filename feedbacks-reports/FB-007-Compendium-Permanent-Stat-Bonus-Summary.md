# FB-007: Add Permanent Stat Bonus Summary to Compendium Progression Tab

## Problem Description
Following the latest update, the **Compendium -> Progression** tab now displays permanent stat bonuses unlocked at each level for a given profession (e.g., *+0.1% Foraging Speed*, *+1 Foraging Power*). 

However, players can currently only view these unlocks sequentially level-by-level in the unlock feed. There is no cumulative summary or breakdown displaying the total aggregate stat bonuses earned from that specific profession to date, forcing players to manually tally up their total permanent stats across 60+ levels.

---

## Proposed Solution
Enhance the individual profession view within the **Compendium -> Progression** menu by adding a cumulative stat summary panel:

1. **Top-Level Cumulative Summary Header:** Position a clean summary header at the top of the profession view (above the individual level unlock list) displaying the sum of all currently active permanent stat gains (e.g., *Total Earned: +3.5% Foraging Speed, +6 Foraging Power, +0.35 Crit Multiplier*).
2. **Dynamic Scaling:** Automatically update the aggregated values as the player levels up and unlocks higher progression thresholds.

---

## User Experience & Value
* **Instant Progression Visibility:** Gives players an immediate, clear overview of how their progression in a specific profession directly impacts their overall character stats.
* **Eliminates Manual Calculation:** Removes the need for players to scroll through dozens of individual level rewards to compute their active passive bonuses.
