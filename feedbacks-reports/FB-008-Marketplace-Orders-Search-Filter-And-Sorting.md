# FB-008: Add Search Filter Bar and Toggleable Sorting Controls to Marketplace Orders Menu

## Problem Description
In the **Marketplace -> Orders** tab, active buy and sell orders are currently displayed strictly in chronological order (first posted, first listed). 

Players managing numerous active trade listings are forced to manually scroll and scan through long, unsorted lists to locate specific entries when editing or canceling orders. The lack of text filtering or column-based sorting creates significant friction for high-volume market traders and craft managers.

---

## Proposed Solution
Enhance the **Marketplace Orders** UI tab with robust filtering and dynamic column sorting tools:

1. **Text Search Filter:** Add an "Item Search" input field at the top of the Orders tab to instantly filter listed active buy and sell orders by item name.
2. **Interactive Column Sorting:** Enable toggleable sorting for the list headers to allow players to organize active listings by:
   * **Alphabetical Order:** Name (A–Z / Z–A)
   * **Item Tier:** Tier (T1–T10)
   * **Price / Unit Value:** Low to High / High to Low
   * **Quantity / Amount:** Highest / Lowest active order count
   * **Date / Post Time:** Newest / Oldest (Default)

---

## User Experience & Value
* **Massive QoL for Market Traders:** Eliminates tedious manual scanning when canceling or updating prices across dozens of active market listings.
* **Streamlined Economic Management:** Enables crafters and arbitrage traders to quickly audit their total active inventory listings and capital distribution by sorting by total value or item tier.
