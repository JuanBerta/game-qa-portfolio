# FB-009: Extend Mud Cave Collapse Timer and Enable Deployable/Mount Access

## Problem Description
Currently, Mud Caves operate on a 2-minute collapse timer. Combined with the restriction preventing deployables and mounts from entering Mud Caves across all tiers, this limited duration forces players to explore and gather strictly on foot at base movement speed. 

As a result, players struggle to clear or harvest valuable nodes inside before the cave collapses, severely slowing down cave progression and resource throughput.

---

## Proposed Solution
Introduce two key adjustments to Mud Cave mechanics across all tiers:

1. **Extended Collapse Duration:** Increase the despawn/collapse timer of Mud Caves from 2 minutes to **4–5 minutes**, allowing sufficient time for thorough exploration and resource extraction.
2. **Deployable & Mount Accessibility:** Allow mounts and deployable vehicles/containers to pass through Mud Cave entrances and function inside cave instances, enabling faster movement and inventory hauling.

---

## User Experience & Value
* **Improved Gathering Efficiency:** Players can fully utilize specialized gear and mounts to haul resources out of caves before the instance despawns.
* **Better Gameplay Pace:** Reduces unnecessary time stress and back-and-forth friction during short-lived cave events.
