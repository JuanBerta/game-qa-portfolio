# FB-011: Add Self-Crafted and Self-Gathered Profit Margin Calculations to Deals Page

## Problem Description
The **Deals** page effectively highlights direct market arbitrage opportunities (buy-low, sell-high between market orders). 

However, it currently evaluates profitability based exclusively on purchasing finished items or raw inputs directly off the marketplace. Crafters and gatherers who produce items using self-sourced materials cannot easily calculate their net profit margin vs. raw input value without manually calculating recipe trees and material costs off-site.

---

## Proposed Solution
Enhance the **Deals** page with self-sourcing calculation toggles or dedicated data columns:

1. **Self-Crafting Profit Column:** Display the projected profit margin when crafting the target trade item from its base components rather than buying the finished good.
2. **Self-Gathered / Zero-Cost Material Toggle:** Allow users to toggle material input costs between *Market Price* and *Self-Gathered ($0 base cost)* to calculate pure labor/time value return.
3. **Recipe Tree Breakdown Integration:** Factor in estimated crafting station fees or refining losses where applicable to provide an accurate net yield comparison.

---

## User Experience & Value
* **Actionable Insight for Crafters & Gatherers:** Helps non-arbitrage players instantly identify which market orders yield the highest return on invested crafting time and gather loops.
* **Deeper Market Analytics:** Complements simple market flips with true production-value metrics, expanding Bitjita's utility for the broader player economy.
