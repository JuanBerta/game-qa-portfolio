# FB-012: Implement Combat Mechanics, Upgradeable Stats, and Unique Skills for Pets

## Problem Description
Pets currently serve as purely cosmetic companions with no active gameplay or combat functionality. 

When gatherers encounter hostile fauna in the wild, they are forced to disengage, swap into combat gear, clear the threat, and then re-equip gathering gear while waiting for stamina to regenerate. This repeated gear-swapping loop disrupts gathering flow and creates tedious downtime during open-world exploration.

---

## Proposed Solution
Expand the pet system to include combat utility, stat progression, and defensive mechanics:

1. **Combat Integration & Targetability:** Allow pets to actively assist in combat. Pets possess independent health pools (HP) and base stats (Damage, Accuracy) and can be targeted and downed by enemies. Once downed, pets enter a temporary recovery cooldown before respawning at full health.
2. **Stat Progression & Unique Abilities:** Enable players to upgrade pet base stats and unlock creature-specific abilities (e.g., damage-over-time poison, AoE cleave, or taunt/aggro control).
3. **Slayer Experience Synergy:** Attribute combat damage dealt by pets toward the player's Slayer skill progression.

---

## User Experience & Value
* **Gatherer Protection:** Provides active defense for gatherers against ambient wild threats without forcing constant gear swaps.
* **Balanced Defensive Utility:** Making pets targetable with finite HP prevents passive "free damage" and rewards tactical positioning and pet upkeep.
* **Expanded PvE Depth:** Offers dungeon runners and solo adventurers additional customization and combat support strategies.
