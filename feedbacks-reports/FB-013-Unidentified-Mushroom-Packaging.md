# FB-013: Enable Packaging for Unidentified Mushrooms Across All Tiers

## Problem Description
Currently, unidentified mushrooms cannot be packaged into crates or bulk containers for efficient transport. 

When combined with the current requirement to use tier-matched Foraging Stations for identification, players gathering high-tier mushrooms in remote biomes are forced to devote excessive individual inventory slots to unstackable/unpackaged raw mushrooms. This creates severe logistical friction during long-distance gathering runs.

---

## Proposed Solution
Allow **unidentified mushrooms of all tiers** to be packaged at a Tier 1 (or higher) Foraging Station:

1. **Unidentified Mushroom Packaging:** Enable packaging recipes for unidentified mushroom variants across all tiers so they can be bundled into trade crates.
2. **Preserve Skill Level Gates:** Maintain the required Foraging skill level threshold for identifying the packaged contents once unpacked, ensuring level progression remains intact.

---

## User Experience & Value
* **Streamlined Exploration Logistics:** Allows gatherers to bulk-pack raw yields in the field and transport large batches back to central processing hubs without clogging personal inventory space.
* **Complements Inventory Workflow:** Aligns unidentified mushroom handling with other raw foraging and resource haul loops.
