# FB-014: Add Audio Cues and Trader Stand Completion Notifications for Marketplace Orders

## Problem Description
Currently, when a global marketplace order is fulfilled in-game, a silent visual notification is displayed. Players engaged in active gameplay (such as gathering, crafting, or combat) frequently miss these visual alerts due to the lack of an accompanying sound effect. 

Furthermore, player-owned **Trader Stands** provide neither visual notifications nor audio cues when an order is completed. This forces players to periodically interrupt gameplay or rely on third-party tracking tools (such as Bitjita) just to check local claim balances and transaction statuses.

---

## Proposed Solution
Implement dedicated audio cues and expand local trade notifications across both market systems:

1. **Marketplace Audio Cues:** Add a distinct, satisfying sound cue when a global Buy or Sell market order is filled or partially completed.
2. **Trader Stand Notifications:** Implement an on-screen pop-up notification for local Trader Stand transactions when a player's posted item is sold or bought.
3. **Trader Stand Audio Cues:** Pair Trader Stand order fulfillments with a clear audio alert so players nearby or in the field immediately know when local inventory changes.

---

## User Experience & Value
* **Improved Market Awareness:** Audio cues ensure players never miss real-time trade completions while focused on other open-world activities.
* **Reduces Third-Party Reliance:** Eliminates the need to constantly monitor external tools or manually open trading menus to verify local shop sales.
