# FB-015: Expand Native Marketplace UI with Global Regional Search, Cross-Region Arbitrage, and Historical Analytics

## Problem Description
The default in-game Marketplace interface limits trade order visibility strictly to local or region-bound queries. 

To evaluate global market trends, spot regional price discrepancies, plan trade routes, or calculate crafting profitability across multi-tier refining chains, players are forced to constantly tab out of the client to use third-party tools like *Bitjita*. Integrating native high-density market analytics directly into the game client would drastically improve economic accessibility and keep player engagement within the primary interface.

---

## Proposed Solution
Overhaul the native **Market Search** interface to incorporate comprehensive global market tools inspired by external economic suites:

1. **Global & Multi-Region Order Aggregation:** Allow players to toggle search scope between local settlements, specific regions, or all global regions to view complete Buy/Sell order books simultaneously.
2. **Cross-Region Arbitrage & Best Deals Display:** Feature explicit "Best Sell" (Lowest Price) and "Best Buy" (Highest Price) metrics alongside region origin indicators to highlight direct trade route opportunities (e.g., *Buy at Location X -> Sell at Location Y*).
3. **Historical Price & Volume Analytics:** Integrate interactive price history tabs displaying average unit prices, lowest historic sell listings, highest buy bids, bid-ask spreads, and 24-hour traded volume.
4. **End-to-End Crafting Profitability Metrics:** Add integrated crafting cost calculators that break down net profit margins from raw inputs to finished goods across any stage of production.

---

## User Experience & Value
* **Native Economic Depth:** Provides traders, crafters, and logistics specialists with professional-grade market intelligence directly inside the client UI.
* **Eliminates External Tool Friction:** Minimizes reliance on web browsers or third-party APIs during active trading and crafting sessions.
