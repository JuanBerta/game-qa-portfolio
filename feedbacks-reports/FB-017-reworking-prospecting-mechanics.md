# FB-017: Reworking Prospecting Loop and High Breadcrumb Requirements

## Problem Description
Prospecting currently requires a tedious, multi-step process. Players must estimate the position of intermediate clues via a directional ring visualizer activated upon using a prospecting ability, repeating this until enough breadcrumbs are registered to point toward the final resource location.

High-tier nodes (e.g., *Argent Ore Prospecting*) require a very high Breadcrumb Count (10–12 steps). This creates an overly active, time-consuming loop that offers disproportionately low rewards for the effort required.

---

## Feedback & Proposed Solutions

### Option 1: Always-Active Precision Ring & Lower Breadcrumb Thresholds
* **Always-Active Directional Ring:** Keep the directional ring visualizer continuously active on the player character during prospecting rather than requiring repeated ability activations.
* **Precise Clue Tracking:** Ensure the active ring always points directly toward the exact location of the next clue node.
* **Reduce Breadcrumb Thresholds:** Lower the maximum Breadcrumb Count requirement across high-tier prospecting activities to a more reasonable range.

---

### Option 2: Single-Use Profession Essence Items
* **Direct Resource Spawning:** Introduce single-use consumable items (e.g., *Mushroom Essence*, *Argent Ore Essence*) for prospecting activities that bypass the clue-tracking phase and immediately reveal the spawned resource node location.
* **Gathering Activity Drops:** Allow players to obtain these consumables as rare drop rewards from standard profession gathering tasks (e.g., harvesting higher-tier nodes increases the probability of acquiring corresponding essences).

---

## Expected Impact
* Reduces tedious downtime and repetitive tracking loops during resource gathering.
* Rebalances high-tier prospecting effort with meaningful progression rewards.
